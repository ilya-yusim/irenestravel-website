Hawaii

Hawaii, matched to the way you travel.

Hawaii vacation planning: which island fits you, where to stay, and how to build the trip around what matters most.

Which island?

This is the first and most important decision. Oahu brings Waikiki energy, history and dining; Maui blends beaches with upcountry charm; the Big Island offers volcanoes, coffee country and dramatic landscape variety; Kauai is the quiet, lush escape. Many travelers do best on one island done well; island-hopping works when the trip is long enough to earn the travel days.

Where to stay matters more in Hawaii than almost anywhere

Two resorts on the same island can deliver completely different vacations — beach quality, pool scene, dining, noise level and value vary enormously. I compare specific properties against what matters to you rather than booking a name you recognize from advertising.

Honeymoons and anniversaries

Hawaii is a classic honeymoon destination for a reason, but "romantic" means different things to different couples — seclusion, adventure, great food, or a mix. I match the island and resort to your version, including room categories worth the splurge and ones that aren't.

Families

Multi-room layouts, kid-friendly beaches with calm water, resorts with real programming for different ages — family Hawaii trips have moving parts, and getting the logistics right is most of the battle.

When to go

Hawaii is genuinely year-round, but seasons still matter: winter brings bigger surf (and whales), summer brings calmer water and warmer temperatures. Rates and crowds follow school calendars closely — timing flexibility is the single biggest lever on price.
