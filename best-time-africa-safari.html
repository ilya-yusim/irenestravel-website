When Is the Best Time to Go on Safari?

The best time depends on where you are going, what you want to see, weather preferences, crowds and budget. A month that is ideal in one region may not be ideal in another.

Wildlife vs weather

Dry-season wildlife concentration is only one factor. Green seasons can bring beautiful landscapes, young animals and different pricing.

Crowds matter too

Peak periods can be excellent for wildlife but busier and more expensive.

Start with your dates

If your travel window is fixed, I work backward from those dates rather than forcing a generic “best month.”
