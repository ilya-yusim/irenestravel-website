Is an African Safari Really That Expensive?

Safari cost depends heavily on season, lodge level, transfers, country and how many people share private services. A well-designed itinerary can prioritize the experiences that matter most without automatically choosing the most expensive option.

What changes the price

Season, lodge category, internal flights, private transfers, park fees and the number of stops can all move the total.

Where value matters

A more expensive lodge is not automatically better for every traveler. Location, guide quality, wildlife access and trip flow matter.

Ask what is included

Safari pricing can include meals, game drives, transfers and some drinks, so comparing only the room rate is misleading.
