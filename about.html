Why Africa became a major focus

After experiencing South Africa and Victoria Falls firsthand, Africa became one of the destinations I most wanted to help clients understand and experience for themselves. I use trusted local partners when an itinerary benefits from strong on-the-ground expertise.

How working with me works

It starts with a conversation — by phone, email or video — about what you want the trip to feel like, not just where you want to go. I then research and compare real options: specific resorts, room categories, cruise cabins, safari lodges, flight routings. You get my honest recommendation with the reasoning behind it, not a list of prices to decode alone. Then I handle the booking, the details and the what-ifs, and I'm here if anything needs attention before or during your trip.

My planning style

I do not believe that the least expensive option is automatically the best option, or that the most expensive one is automatically better. I look at the details that change the actual trip.
