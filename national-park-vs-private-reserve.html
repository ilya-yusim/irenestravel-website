National Park vs Private Reserve: What’s the Difference?

Both can deliver excellent wildlife viewing, but rules, vehicle traffic and activities can differ. The right choice depends on the destination and the type of safari you want.

National parks

Often larger public protected areas with established rules and routes.

Private reserves and concessions

May offer different levels of vehicle access, off-road driving or additional activities depending on the property and destination.

Mixing both

Some itineraries benefit from combining different safari environments rather than choosing only one type.
