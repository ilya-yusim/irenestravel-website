Can you combine safari with a beach vacation?

Absolutely — but the best combination depends on where you safari, how much flight time you want and what kind of beach experience you expect. The golden rule: pair the beach with the safari region that reaches it most easily, and don't underestimate how good "doing nothing with a view" feels after early-morning game drives.

Zanzibar

A natural East Africa pairing, especially when the safari portion is in Tanzania. Arusha to Zanzibar is a short hop, and the island offers everything from lively beach towns to quiet hideaways. This is the classic safari-beach combination and the easiest to get right.

Mozambique

A strong fit for travelers who want a beach extension that still feels connected to Southern Africa. Pristine beaches and excellent diving, reached via Johannesburg — it pairs well with a South Africa safari.

Mauritius

A polished resort stay that can work well after safari when air routing makes sense. Great for honeymoons — calm lagoons, top-tier service and easy connections from Johannesburg.

Seychelles

Beautiful and romantic, but routing and total travel time deserve careful attention. Best when the budget and schedule allow a true splurge finale — often paired with East Africa via Nairobi.

Cape Town

Not a tropical beach extension, but an excellent coastal city finish for travelers who want scenery, dining and variety. After a Kruger safari, a few days in Cape Town can be the perfect exhale.

Safari first, beach second

I almost always put the safari first and the beach second. Safari days start early and ask a lot of you; the beach is the reward. Ending relaxed also means you fly home rested instead of exhausted.
