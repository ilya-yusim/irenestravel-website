What Should You Wear on Safari?

Comfort, layers and practical fabrics matter more than looking like you bought a safari costume. Most travelers need fewer special items than they expect.

Layers

Early game drives can feel much cooler than midday.

Neutral and practical

Comfortable, breathable clothing in subdued colors is easy to rewear.

Luggage limits

Small aircraft can have strict luggage rules, so packing strategy matters as much as wardrobe.
