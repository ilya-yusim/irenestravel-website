First Safari

First African safari: where do you start?

A first safari is easier to plan once you understand a few big decisions: country, reserve type, season, pace, lodge style and how much flying or driving you are comfortable with.

Country comes first

South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania and other safari destinations can deliver very different trip styles. I start by matching the destination to the experience you want.

Reserve type matters

Private reserves, national parks and concessions can differ in crowd levels, activities and flexibility. There is no single “best” choice for everyone.

Do not over-move

More lodges do not automatically mean a better safari. A good itinerary leaves enough time to settle in and actually experience each place.

Budget is about priorities

Lodge category, season, transfers and the number of safari areas all affect cost. I focus on where spending more changes the experience and where it may not.
