South Africa

South Africa: safari plus so much more.

South Africa can combine wildlife, Cape Town, wine country, coast and excellent lodging in one itinerary, which makes it especially appealing for a first Africa trip.

Safari

Private reserves and national parks offer multiple ways to structure the wildlife portion of a trip.

Cape Town

A city stay adds food, scenery, neighborhoods, coastline and an easy change of pace after safari.

Wine country

Stellenbosch and Franschhoek are natural additions for travelers who enjoy food, wine and beautiful scenery.

Good first-Africa fit

South Africa works especially well for travelers who want safari but do not want every day of the trip to be safari.
